## About

Born in the heart of Kishangarh, StarBrew Cafe is where specialty coffee meets small-town charm. Every cup is crafted with care — from rich espressos to signature frappes — using quality beans and creative blends.

More than just coffee, StarBrew is a morning ritual, a study corner, and a catch-up spot for the community.

> "Where every sip tells a story"
